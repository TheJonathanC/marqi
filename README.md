# Marqi

A personal streaming launcher for India. Browse, filter, and launch movies and TV shows on **Prime Video**, **JioHotstar**, and **Apple TV+** without opening the heavy official apps.

- India-only (`IN`) catalog, subscription (`flatrate`) titles only: no rentals, purchases, or paid add-on channels
- Metadata and artwork from TMDB, streaming offers and deep links from JustWatch
- Smart redirect layer that cleans links and guards against non-India storefronts
- Continue Watching, powered by a dwell-time heuristic
